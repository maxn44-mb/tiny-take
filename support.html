Support

Most answers are in the app. Open Settings, then Help: there is a short article for nearly everything, and the tour again.

Get in touch

For anything else, tap Send feedback in Tiny Take’s Settings, or write to max.nichols44@gmail.com. Every message is read.

Where are my seconds kept?

On your iPhone, in Tiny Take’s own private storage, and with iCloud Backup on, in your own iCloud too. Encrypted in your iCloud. Tiny Take can’t see it.

Will they come with me to a new iPhone?

Yes. With iCloud Backup on, sign in to iCloud on the new iPhone, install Tiny Take and open it: it finds your seconds, and Restore brings them back. Without it, they are part of your iPhone’s own backup, so setting up a new iPhone from that backup brings them back.

Missed a day?

Everyone does. There are no streaks here and nobody’s counting. If you took a photo or a video that day, bring it in from the Timeline and it goes to its own day.

How do I take everything out?

In Settings, tap Export everything. It saves every second as a file, wherever you choose.

Can I add another baby?

Yes. Tap the picture at the top of Today, then Add a profile. Each baby keeps their own seconds, tags and films, and a moment of two of them together is saved once for both.

Something isn’t working

Tap Send feedback in Settings and say what happened. The email says which version you have, which helps to find the fault.
