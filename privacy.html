Privacy Policy

Everything stays on your iPhone. Tiny Take has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers, and it never connects to the internet.

What Tiny Take keeps, and where

Everything you make in Tiny Take is kept in its own private storage on your iPhone. That is the seconds you film or bring in, each baby’s name, birthday, photo and colour, your tags and notes, the films you make and the words you put on them, and your settings.

None of it is sent to us. Tiny Take has no servers, so there is nowhere for it to go.

The widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen are given a small summary to show, such as a name, an age and a few small pictures from the last week. It stays on your iPhone too, where only Tiny Take and its widgets can read it.

What your iPhone does with your seconds

Tiny Take can suggest tags, such as Smile or Laugh, by going through seconds you have already saved, their pictures and their sound. Your iPhone does this itself, with tools built into iOS, and what it finds stays on your iPhone. Where your iPhone has Apple Intelligence, Tiny Take uses the part that works on the iPhone itself, never Apple’s servers.

Tiny Take never notes crying, anything to do with health, or when a baby was asleep.

When you hide the faces in a film, your iPhone finds them. Tiny Take keeps only where the covers go: never a picture of a face, and nothing that could say whose it is.

A film you ask for in words is read on your iPhone in the same way.

What Tiny Take asks to use

  • Camera and microphone, to film a second. They are used only while Tiny Take’s camera is open.
  • Photos, to bring in the photos and videos you choose, and to save a film there when you ask.
  • Notifications, if you allow them, for a reminder to film and for milestones. They are planned on your iPhone.
  • Siri and Shortcuts, if you use them to film a second. Siri hears your request under Apple’s own privacy policy, and Tiny Take is only told which baby to film.

You can change any of these at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

Sharing, and taking everything out

A film leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself, to wherever you choose. What happens to it after that is up to the app or the person you send it to.

Export everything, in Tiny Take’s Settings, saves every second as a file, wherever you choose. Your seconds are yours.

Backups

Your seconds are included in your iPhone’s own backups, to iCloud or to a computer, so they come with you to a new iPhone. Those backups are made and kept by Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Deleting

When you delete a second or a baby’s profile, Tiny Take keeps it for 30 days in case you change your mind, and then removes it for good.

Deleting Tiny Take from your iPhone deletes everything it kept there. Copies in your iPhone’s backups last until those backups are replaced.

Children

Tiny Take is made for parents, not for children to use. We do not collect information about anyone, children included.

If you write to us

If you send feedback, from Tiny Take’s Settings or to the address below, we see your email address and what you write. The email also says which version of Tiny Take and of iOS you have, unless you take that out. We use it only to reply and to make Tiny Take better, and we never share it.
